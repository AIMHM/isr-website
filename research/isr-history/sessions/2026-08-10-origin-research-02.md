# H032 — Official RMIT Muslim student community evidence

YEAR:
1997

SOURCE TYPE:
Official RMIT University annual report.

GRADE:
A

FINDING:
RMIT's 1997 Annual Report identifies international engineering
student Rini Akmeliawati and states that:

"She continues to be active in the RMIT Muslim student community."

The report records that she completed a Bachelor of Engineering
(Electrical) and won the Patricia Guthrie Award.

SOURCE:
RMIT University Annual Report 1997

URL:
https://www.rmit.edu.au/assets/documents/about/Governance-and-management/annual-reports/1997-annual-report.pdf

ADDITIONAL BIOGRAPHICAL LEAD:
Rini's public academic profile gives her RMIT study period as
February 1993 – March 1997.

URL:
https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Rini-Akmeliawati/3

SUPPORTS:
An identifiable RMIT Muslim student community existed by 1997.

DOES NOT PROVE:
- that Rini was formally an RMIT Islamic Society officer/member;
- that the formal Society was founded in 1993;
- that she was involved in establishing it.

FOLLOW-UP:
Rini is an extremely high-value oral-history lead because her RMIT
study period reaches back to 1993.

# H042 — Building 9 vs Building 11 discrepancy

STATUS:
MAJOR DISCREPANCY — P0.

SOURCE A:
Older Salaam Monash handbook.

URL:
https://www.scribd.com/document/57163200/Salaam

EVIDENCE:
The document contains:
- a campus map dated October 2005;
- a link referring to "2006handbooks";
- an RMIT listing giving:

RMIT City Campus
Corner of Franklin & Bowen St
Building 11, Basement

SOURCE B:
Deakin Islamic Directory 2007.

URL:
https://www.yumpu.com/en/document/view/6113598/islamic-directory-project786

EVIDENCE:
RMIT prayer room:
Building 9 Level 4.

SOURCE C:
Later prayer-room campaign research describes the long-standing
Building 9 Level 4 dedicated facility and the Building 11 replacement
arrangements involved in the late-2007 dispute.

URL:
https://archive.sa.org.au/mi/2/mi2wardwood.pdf

POSSIBLE EXPLANATIONS:
1. Salaam Monash contained incorrect RMIT information.
2. Building 11 was already used alongside Building 9.
3. Building 11 was a temporary/additional facility.
4. The surviving handbook was partly updated after its original
   layout/maps were produced.
5. Different publications used "prayer room" to mean different
   spaces.
6. The historical chronology currently understood from later
   campaign sources is incomplete.

DO NOT SILENTLY RECONCILE.

REQUIRED EVIDENCE:
Original RMIT facilities records and dated Muslim student guides.

# CURRENT WORKING CONCLUSION AFTER SESSION 02

The current evidence supports a Muslim student community at RMIT by
at least 1997 and increasingly strong evidence of organised Islamic
Society activity by the late 1990s.

The exact founding date remains unverified.

The mid-2000s record contains a significant unresolved identity issue:
"RMIT Islamic Society" appears in RMIT Student Union material while
external Muslim student directories list "United Muslim University
Students" c/o RMIT Student Union.

The same 9925 2437 phone number later appears for RMITIS, creating a
continuity clue that could indicate organisational succession or simply
shared Student Union infrastructure.

The prayer-space chronology also contains a major unresolved
Building 9 / Building 11 conflict.

Neither question should be silently reconciled.

They are now priority historical research problems.
