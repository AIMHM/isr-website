# ISR Historical Research Archive

This directory is the durable research archive for the history of:

- Muslim student life at RMIT
- RMIT Islamic Society
- RMITIS
- Islamic Society of RMIT (ISR)
- Muslim prayer facilities at RMIT
- RMIT Bundoora Islamic Society
- ISR/RMIT relationships
- ISR leadership and committees
- Islamic programs, publications and events
- Advocacy and representation
- Inter-MSA and wider community activity
- ISR's digital history

## Core rule

Never discard evidence merely because it is weak.

Weak evidence is preserved, graded, and followed up.

Do not silently convert:
- recollection into fact;
- inference into fact;
- an external allegation into an ISR finding;
- one historical committee's political position into permanent ISR policy;
- a prayer-room establishment date into the Society's founding date.

## Current founding-date status

A current website claims ISR was founded in 1993.

That claim is preserved as a research lead, but no primary founding
constitution, registration record, AGM record, club affiliation record
or contemporaneous RMIT record has yet been recovered to verify it.

Do not publish "Founded 1993" as established fact until primary or
sufficiently corroborated historical evidence is recovered.

## Version control

This research archive is stored in Git.

Every substantial research session should end with:

1. updating the evidence register;
2. updating affected people/place/program files;
3. adding unresolved questions;
4. recording new dead-web/archive targets;
5. making a local Git checkpoint.

Nothing from this directory should be pushed or deployed without
separate explicit approval.
