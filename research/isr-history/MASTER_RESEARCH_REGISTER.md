PERIOD:
Approximately early 1990s to late 2007.

CLAIM:

facility demolished in late 2007 had existed for almost fifteen years.

GRADE:
B

SUPPORTS:

approximately 1992/93–94.

"The RMIT University Muslim Prayer Room Campaign 2008–2009"

URL:
https://archive.sa.org.au/mi/2/mi2wardwood.pdf

1991–1995.

# MASTER CURRENT CONCLUSION

The evidence currently supports the following careful historical
position:

Muslim student life at RMIT has a documented history spanning
decades.

Dedicated Muslim prayer infrastructure appears to reach back to
approximately the early 1990s.

RMIT officially documented an active Muslim student community in
1997.

Biographical evidence identifies RMIT Islamic Society editorial
activity spanning 1996–1999.

An external Islamic web index last modified in February 1998 already
linked directly to an RMIT-hosted page identified as:
"RMIT, Islamic Society."

By 1999–2000 a biographical record identifies an Executive Committee
and Publication Wing.

By 2003 academic research documents RMITIS maintaining a website,
Islamic resources, a multimedia centre, lectures, conferences,
video programs and Islamic Awareness Week.

The exact founding year remains unresolved.

1993 is a serious research lead but not yet a verified founding date.
