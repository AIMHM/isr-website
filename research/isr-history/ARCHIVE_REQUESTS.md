# Archive Request Plan

## RMIT University Archives — first request

Research objective:

Trace the establishment and early development of the RMIT Islamic
Society / Muslim student community and dedicated Muslim prayer
facilities.

Priority date range:
1990–2000.

Request whether the Archives hold:

- student club/society affiliation registers;
- RMIT Union records;
- Student Union records;
- clubs and societies handbooks;
- constitutions;
- club registration/renewal records;
- committee/election records;
- student diaries;
- orientation handbooks;
- international student guides;
- Muslim student guides;
- "Salaam RMIT";
- prayer-room allocation records;
- Building 9 Level 4 records;
- chaplaincy/religious-services records;
- correspondence concerning Muslim student facilities;
- photographs;
- Catalyst references;
- room-booking/facilities records.

Secondary range:
2001–2010.

Seek:
- RMITIS records;
- Building 9 demolition;
- Building 11 Spiritual Centre;
- 2008–09 prayer-room campaign correspondence;
- RMIT media releases;
- Student Union Council documents;
- NTEU correspondence;
- Islamic Council of Victoria correspondence.

## Alumni oral-history targets

Begin with people who can potentially bridge the evidence gaps:

- late-1990s editors/publication officers;
- Rini Akmeliawati;
- Nasreen Badruddin;
- Noor Hasbi Yusoff;
- early-2000s office holders;
- Salaam RMIT contributors;
- 2007–09 RMITIS leadership;
- Bundoora Islamic Society leadership;
- former chaplains.

Do not ask leading questions about a supposed 1993 foundation.
Ask open questions first:
"What do you remember about when the Society began?"
"What existed when you arrived?"
"Who was already involved?"
"What documents did you inherit?"
